    Comms Ops panel (Star Comms beta)¶

    The Comms Ops page shows your org's Star Comms shard live in the web portal: whether the operation is open, which nets exist, and who is on each one. Officers can also run the op from here: assign people to nets, open or close the operation, send ACARS messages, save and apply presets, and set role rules that put everyone with a Discord role on a net.

    You'll find it under Events & Ops → Comms Ops. It appears once your server has linked its Star Comms shard (Server Settings → External) and is in the Star Comms beta. The same things are available in Discord through the /comms commands.

    Running an op on Star Comms¶

    Every event and template has a Voice comms choice: Discord, Star Comms or Both. In the event wizard it is the Voice Comms step after the roster, and on a server with Star Comms linked it is the one place for every voice setting: the Meet Up channel and net, and each team's Discord channel and net (the roster step is just teams and slots). On the full event form and the template form it sits with the voice settings. Star Comms and Both are offered once the shard is linked (below) and your server is in the beta.

    • Discord — voice channels in your server, exactly as before.
    • Star Comms — each team gets a Star Comms net.
    • Both — Discord channels and Star Comms nets side by side.

    Pick a Meet Up net for the whole op and a net for each team: one of your shard's existing nets, Create a new net… with a name, or no net. A recurring op or one created from a template gets the template's choice. The event's Discord announcement then shows a Comms section with the Meet Up net and each team's net and a Get Star Comms link and button. The link goes to the download link from the settings card, or the Star Comms website when none is set. On a Star Comms-only op the announcement no longer shows the Discord "Meetup Channel" line — the Comms section's Meet Up → net is where people gather (a Both op shows the two). If the shard is later unlinked, the section quietly disappears and nothing else about the event changes.

    Teams and nets can also be changed from the event page: open the op, and in Mission Structure press Manage Nets (Manage Voice on a Both op). A Meet Up net picker for the whole op sits above the teams, and each team has a Team net picker, both with the same choices as the wizard; a Star Comms-only op hides the Discord channel pickers there, and Fleet Command shows each team's net instead of its voice channel. Outside the editor, Mission Structure shows the op's Meet Up → net above the teams.

    New nets are created on your shard when the op is saved (it takes a few seconds; "(new)" in the announcement turns into the real net). If the shard is down or refuses — for example a plan that doesn't include creating nets — the op saves anyway and Star Org tries again on the next save and when the op launches. A net Star Org created is removed from the shard when the op ends, is cancelled or is deleted — unless another upcoming op still uses it, which then takes it over — and nets you picked that already existed are never touched. Copying an op gives the copy its own new nets. If the shard is down when an op is deleted outright, its nets stay on the shard.

    Putting people on their nets. Everyone signed up to a Star Comms (or Both) op is assigned on your shard to their team's net and the op's Meet Up net — a few seconds after they sign up, swap team or ship, or are moved by an officer, and again whenever a new net is created for the op. Tentative and late signups count; benched, pending and declined ones don't. Members from allied servers are assigned too. Star Comms lets a member sit on several nets, so this only adds nets: someone who was already on a net keeps it, and when they withdraw, change team, or the op ends or is cancelled, Star Org takes back only the nets it gave them — never one they already had or one another upcoming op still gives them. A member the shard doesn't know (not on your Star Comms roster yet) is skipped and everyone else is still assigned; they're picked up on the next change after they join. If the shard is down nothing is lost: the next signup change tries again.

    A global template (made by the Star Org team) can carry a Star Comms choice and "create a net" requests, but not your shard's existing nets; a copy into a server without Star Comms falls back to Discord.

    Linking the shard (server admins)¶

    In Server Settings → External → Star Comms Shard:

    1. Shard URL. Hosted shards live at https://s-<your-org>.star-comms.org — note the s-. The same address without s- is the Star Comms website, not your shard; if you enter it, the card says so and offers the right address in one click. The field is forgiving: a Star Comms launch link, a bare host (s-yourorg.star-comms.org), a host with a port, or an address with a path are all reduced to the shard address, and a short note under the field says what was trimmed. A launch link's id and token are never kept.
    2. Owner API key. Your shard admin creates a scok_… key in the shard's Advanced API Integration settings. The card's Required permissions list shows every scope the key needs, what each one is for, and which are optional (marked for planned features).
    3. Test Connection checks the address is a Star Comms shard and that the key works. Verify permissions checks each scope it can without changing anything on your shard and shows Granted, Missing, Key rejected or Can't check beside it. Write scopes (assigning, ACARS, nets, rules…) can't be checked without making a change, so they show Can't check; make sure the key has them. If every scope says Missing, the key may have none of them — or the shard is refusing this server's IP address. When the shard reports its plan limits (how many nets, whether ACARS is on), they are shown under the list.

    Both checks are limited to a few a minute; if you hit the limit the buttons wait until you can try again.

    What you see¶

    • Four tiles across the top:
      • Operation: open, closed, or unknown (the shard has not said yet).
      • Connected: how many people are on comms.
      • Nets: how many nets the shard reports.
      • Freshness: how current the page is (see below), with how long ago the data was updated.
    • One card per net listing who is on it. A blue badge with a microphone means that person is transmitting right now. People on no net, or on a net the shard no longer lists, appear under Unassigned.
    • The last ACARS message and who sent it.

    Names come from your Discord server: the member's server nickname, else their username. Someone who is not a member of your server shows the name the shard has for them, or their Discord ID if it has none. Members who have hidden themselves from the roster are not named.

    How fresh is it? (about 10 seconds behind)¶

    The page refreshes about every 10 seconds. It does not ask your shard directly. It reads the copy the bot keeps from the shard's live event stream. So when someone joins a net, changes channel or keys their mic, expect the page to catch up within about 10 seconds. That delay is normal, not a fault. Your own changes (an assignment, opening the operation) are sent to the shard straight away; the page shows them on its next refresh.

    The Freshness tile tells you how far to trust what you see:

    Freshness Meaning
    Live The bot is connected to the shard and the data is current.
    Stale The bot has not reported for a few minutes (it may be reconnecting or just starting up). The page shows the last data it has, which may be out of date.
    Link error The bot cannot reach the shard, or the shard is refusing it (for example a rejected API key). The reason is shown, and the page keeps the last roster it saw.
    Not set up The server has no working Star Comms link. A server admin sets it up in Server Settings → External.

    Officer controls¶

    These need the Comms Management permission (officers by default). Members see the panel read-only.

    • Open operation / Close operation. Asks you to confirm first, because everyone on comms sees it.
    • Assign members. Opens a side panel:
      • Choose Assign or Unassign.
      • Pick one or more members. People currently on comms are listed first, then your server's members.
      • For Assign, pick the net. With exactly one member you can also set Temporary (minutes), from 1 to 1440, and the shard removes the assignment when it runs out.
      • Up to 200 members at once. If any net can't be found, nothing is changed.
      • Unassigning several people skips anyone who had no net and tells you how many.
    • ACARS. Type up to 280 characters (the counter shows how many you've used) and choose the alert type: Emergency (the default), Critical or Routine. It is sent to everyone on comms under your server name. Your server can send one ACARS message a minute. After a send, the button shows a countdown until the next one is allowed, and the countdown carries over if you reload the page.

    If the shard refuses an action (a rejected key, a missing permission on the key, a rate limit), the panel shows the reason. Nothing is retried automatically, so an action is never sent twice.

    Presets and role rules (officers)¶

    Officers see three tabs at the top of the page: Live (everything above), Presets and Rules. Members only see the live view. Presets and rules are stored on your shard, not by the bot.

    Presets¶

    A preset is a saved layout: who sits on which net, and whether the operation is open.

    • Save current layout asks for a name and saves the board exactly as the shard has it right now. Saving with the name of an existing preset replaces it (the button says Overwrite preset when it will).
    • Apply shows what the preset will do ("Places 12 members onto Command, Alpha Wing and opens the operation") and asks you to confirm. Members who are not in the preset stay where they are. Applying the same preset twice changes nothing the second time.
    • If a net in the preset has been deleted since it was saved, applying it is refused and nobody is moved. Save the preset again to fix it.
    • Delete removes the preset from the shard. Nobody is moved.

    In Discord, /comms preset list shows the presets and /comms preset apply <name> applies one.

    Role rules¶

    A rule gives a Discord role a net: the shard puts everyone holding that role on it.

    • Each row is a Discord role and a Net. Add rule adds a row; the bin icon removes one. A role can have only one rule.
    • Save rules replaces the whole list on the shard. It does not add to it: any rule you removed from the page is deleted, and the confirmation says how many. Saving an empty list deletes every rule.
    • Only your own server's roles can be used. A rule whose net no longer exists is marked, and must be changed or removed before you can save.
    • Discard changes puts the list back to what the shard has.
